Famous Places to Visit in India

Home | Destinations | Foods | Places | Gallery | Contact

1. Taj Mahal, Agra

The Taj Mahal is a famous marble monument and a symbol of love.

2. India Gate, Delhi

India Gate is a war memorial located in New Delhi.

3. Hawa Mahal, Jaipur

Hawa Mahal is known for its beautiful pink architecture and many windows.

4. Golden Temple, Amritsar

The Golden Temple is a sacred Sikh place of worship in Punjab.

5. Kerala Backwaters

Kerala is famous for its peaceful waterways and beautiful natural scenery.
